Utility monitoring

Home · Water

Home → Water. Fresh install, saved readings offline, sample data, connected, live flow, possible leak and signal warning. The ring warms toward red and the water fills as usage approaches the goal. Niro Summary sits below daily usage.

Home · Gas

Home → Gas. Niro shows that monitoring is unavailable.

Home · Electricity

Home → Electricity. Niro shows that monitoring is unavailable.

Utility reports

Reports follows the selected utility. Water shows daily bars and a breakdown. Gas and Electricity show Niro’s unavailable state.

Household survey

Devices → Start or edit household survey. Also appears after eligible first-time pairing.

Review water use

Home → Review today’s use when available. Shows suggested activities, an empty review or a completed review.

Niro and demos

Niro · Setup

On Water, open Niro and tap Download to use. The 137 MB model runs on your device. On Gas and Electricity, Niro shows that monitoring is unavailable.

Niro · Chat

After setup, send a prompt to get text, an inline chart or a table. New chat clears the temporary thread.

Niro · Today

Niro → Today. Shows the forecast, goal and what’s needed when data is missing.

Niro · Recent use

Niro → Recent use. Shows recent readings and a comparison with earlier measured days.

Demo · Leak detection

Help → Try the app demos → Leak detection. Advance a minute to show the alert, then stop the simulated flow.

Demo · Water trends

Help → Try the app demos → Water trends. Compare sample usage over seven days.

Demo · AI water coach

Help → Try the app demos → AI water coach. See the sample forecast, goal and suggested action.

Device setup and settings

Device settings

Devices. Shows connection status, reconnect, sample-data switch, survey, calibration and history download when supported.

Water meter calibration

Devices → Water meter calibration. Enter pulses per second and the time to collect 160 mL. Review the current conversion and save.

Pair your device

Devices → Pair or reconnect. Camera or QR photo, recognized sticker, Bluetooth chooser, connection feedback, live pulse diagnostics and clear saved readings.

Account and help

Sign-in or phone-only setup

Tap Sign in on Account or pair a device to start setup. Google sign-in and Bluefy phone-only setup have separate paths.

Account

Open Account to adjust app settings. After sign-in or phone-only setup, you can also edit your profile, home name and water goal.

App language

Account → Settings → App Language. Pick a language or return to English.

Appearance

Account → Settings → Appearance. Switch between light and dark.

Help & support

Account → Settings → Help & support. Expand the FAQ or open the demos and this page map.

Quick tour

Help → Take the quick tour. Three steps cover connecting the monitor, setting a goal and asking Niro.

Family Connect

Account → Settings → Family Connect, at the bottom. Sign-in, no group, joined group, invite and join-request states depend on the account.
